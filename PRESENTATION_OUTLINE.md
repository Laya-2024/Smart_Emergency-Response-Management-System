# Smart Emergency Response Management System — presentation

## Slide 1 — Title
Smart Emergency Response Management System: secure, verified, role-based emergency coordination.

## Slide 2 — Problem
Emergency reporting is slow, responders are difficult to coordinate, and public broadcast of sensitive cases can create safety and privacy risks.

## Slide 3 — Solution
One-tap SOS with live location, category-based routing, verified responders, emergency timelines and reliable notifications.

## Slide 4 — Users
Citizens, verified volunteers, doctors, police, dispatchers, shelter managers, donors and administrators.

## Slide 5 — Safety design
Email/mobile verification, document review for sensitive roles, encrypted sensitive data, least-privilege permissions and audit trails.

## Slide 6 — Intelligent alert routing
Women safety → police only. Accidents → nearby verified doctors/volunteers/police. Fire → fire/dispatch/police. Disaster → authorised relief network.

## Slide 7 — Technology
Java, Spring MVC/Security, Hibernate/JPA, MySQL, Flyway, HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

## Slide 8 — Reliability
Idempotent SOS requests, status indexes, notification outbox, delivery records, health probes and escalation rules.

## Slide 9 — Impact
Faster routing, safer information sharing, accountable response coordination and post-resolution service ratings.

## Slide 10 — Future scope
Production SMS/push gateway, map provider, mobile apps, multilingual UI, analytics, load balancing and disaster-recovery deployment.
